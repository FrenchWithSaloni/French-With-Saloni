Data Deletion Instructions

Saloni French Academy Ltd. (operating as "French with Saloni") · Last updated: November 17, 2026

What you can ask us to delete

You can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you, including your WhatsApp conversations, your phone number, and details from our website forms.

How to request deletion

  • By email: write to frenchwithsaloni@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Include your full name and the WhatsApp number you used, with the country code.
  • By WhatsApp: send "Delete my data" to +1 778-882-8553 from the same number you used to contact us.

What happens next

  • We confirm your request by reply and may ask you to verify that the number or email is yours.
  • Your conversation history, contact details and enquiry records are deleted from our systems within 30 days.
  • Some records may be kept where the law requires it, such as invoices and payment records for enrolled students.
  • Copies held by Meta (WhatsApp) follow Meta's own policies, which you can manage in your WhatsApp settings.

For more details, read our Privacy Policy or contact us at frenchwithsaloni@gmail.com.
