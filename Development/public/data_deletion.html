Data Deletion Instructions

Saloni French Academy Ltd. · Last updated: November 11, 2026

You can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you, including your WhatsApp conversations, your phone number and any details from our website forms.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to frenchwithsaloni@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number you used to message us (with country code, for example +1 778-882-8553) and your full name.
  3. Alternatively, send the message “Delete my data” to us on WhatsApp at +1 778-882-8553 from that same number.

What happens next

  • We confirm your request by reply and may ask you to verify that the number or email is yours.
  • We delete your WhatsApp conversation history, contact details and enquiry records from our systems within 30 days.
  • Some records may be kept where the law requires it, such as invoices and payment records for enrolled students.
  • Copies held by Meta (WhatsApp) are governed by Meta’s own policies; you can manage those in your WhatsApp settings.

Questions? Read our Privacy Policy or write to frenchwithsaloni@gmail.com.
