Refund Policy

Saloni French Academy Ltd. (operating as "French with Saloni") · Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Our policy in short

We do not offer refunds once your batch has started. This applies even if you have attended only one class. Your fee reserves your seat and the teacher's time for the whole batch, so we cannot return it once classes begin.

2. Free demo class

Our demo class is free. We encourage you to attend a demo, and to ask us any questions, before you enrol, so that you are sure the course is right for you.

3. Before your batch starts

If you change your mind before the first class of your batch, write to us and we will tell you whether the fee can be returned or moved to a later batch.

4. If your schedule changes

We understand that work timings and other commitments can change. If a batch no longer suits you, we will do our best to move you to another batch of the same level at no extra cost, subject to availability. We do not issue a refund in this situation.

5. If we cancel or change a class

If we cancel a class, we will reschedule it or offer a make-up session. If we cancel an entire batch before it is completed, we will refund the fee for the classes that were not delivered, or move you to another batch if you prefer.

6. Recordings and digital access

Recordings, notes, quizzes and student portal access are digital products. Once access has been given, they cannot be refunded.

7. Monthly payments

If you choose monthly payment, your card is charged automatically through Stripe on the same date each month until you cancel. By enrolling, you authorise these automatic charges. You will receive an email receipt after each payment.

  • To stop future payments: tell us at least 3 days before your next payment date, by email or WhatsApp. If you tell us later, that month's payment will be taken and will not be refunded.
  • Failed payments: if a payment fails, we will let you know and you will have a few days to fix it. Access to classes and the student portal may be paused until the payment is made.
  • Pausing: we do not pause payments automatically. If you need a break, contact us and we will see what we can arrange.
  • A month that has already been charged is not refunded, even if you stop attending.

8. Payment processing fees

Any card or payment processing fees are non-refundable.
