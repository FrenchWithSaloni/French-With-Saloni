Privacy Policy

Saloni French Academy Ltd. (“Saloni French Academy”, “we”, “us”) · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Saloni French Academy Ltd. runs online French classes for students preparing for TEF/TCF and general French learning, under the name “French with Saloni”. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit frenchwithsaloni.com or message us on WhatsApp, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Saloni French Academy Ltd., British Columbia, Canada. Contact: frenchwithsaloni@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • WhatsApp messages: your WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and the content of the messages you send us (for example your questions about courses and your preferred class times).
  • Website forms: name, email address, phone number, course interest and the message you type in our contact or demo-class forms.
  • Demo class booking: your name, email address and the demo date you choose, so we can send you a calendar invitation and reminder.
  • Class and account information for enrolled students, such as batch, attendance and payment status.

3. How we use your information

  • To answer your questions about our courses and help you book a free demo class.
  • To send you the demo class invitation, reminders and class-related information.
  • To enrol you, manage classes and billing, and provide support.
  • To improve our service and keep our systems secure.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Automated replies and AI

When you message us on WhatsApp, an automated assistant may write the first replies. It uses an AI service (Anthropic’s Claude) to understand your message and draft a response. Your message text is sent to that service for this purpose only. A member of our team can take over the conversation at any time, and the assistant hands over to a person when you ask for one or when it cannot help.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the service, and only as needed:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • Anthropic to generate automated replies.
  • Render (cloud hosting) to run our messaging system.
  • Google Workspace (Calendar, Meet, Sheets, Gmail) to schedule demo classes and manage student records.
  • QuickBooks for invoicing and payments of enrolled students.

These providers may process information outside Canada, including in the United States. We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. How long we keep information

We keep WhatsApp conversations and enquiry details for up to 12 months after your last message, unless you enrol, in which case we keep student records for as long as needed to provide the course and meet our legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your information sooner (see section 8).

7. Security

We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or stop receiving messages from us (reply “STOP” on WhatsApp or email us). To request deletion, follow the steps at frenchwithsaloni.com/data-deletion. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children

Our service is intended for adults and for students whose parents or guardians contact us on their behalf. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
