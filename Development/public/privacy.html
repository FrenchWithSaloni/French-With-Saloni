Privacy Policy

Saloni French Academy Ltd. (operating as "French with Saloni") · Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Who we are

Saloni French Academy Ltd. is based in British Columbia, Canada, and runs online French classes under the name "French with Saloni" (frenchwithsaloni.com). You can contact us at frenchwithsaloni@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +1 778-882-8553.

2. Information we collect

  • WhatsApp: your phone number, profile name and the content of your messages (for example course questions and preferred class times).
  • Website forms: name, email, phone number, course interest and the message you type.
  • Demo class booking: name, email and the demo date you choose.
  • Enrolled students: batch, attendance, class and payment records.
  • Payments: card payments are handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number; we keep a record of what you paid and when.

3. How we use it

We use your information to answer course questions, book free demo classes, send invitations and reminders, enrol students, manage classes and billing, provide support and keep our systems secure. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Automated replies and AI

An automated assistant may write the first replies to your WhatsApp messages. It uses Anthropic's Claude to interpret messages and draft responses. Message text is sent to Anthropic only for this purpose. A team member can take over at any time, and the assistant hands the conversation to a person when you ask or when it cannot help.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the academy, and only as needed:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • Anthropic: to interpret and draft replies (see section 4).
  • Supabase: our database provider, where information from our website (such as form enquiries, demo bookings and student accounts) is stored.
  • Render: hosting for our automated assistant, where WhatsApp conversation records and demo-booking details are stored.
  • Google Workspace: scheduling and records.
  • Stripe: to process card payments, including automatic monthly payments. Stripe collects and stores your card details under its own privacy policy.
  • QuickBooks: invoicing and payments.
  • Resend: to send you emails such as enrolment confirmations and demo class details.
  • Twilio: to send our team a WhatsApp notification when someone enrols or books, including the student's name, email and phone number.

Some of these providers may process information outside Canada, including in the United States. They handle it only to provide their services to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

WhatsApp conversations and enquiry details are kept for up to 12 months after your last message and are then deleted. Records for enrolled students are kept as long as needed to provide the course and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

7. Security

We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, and you can stop messages at any time by replying "STOP" on WhatsApp or by emailing us. Deletion requests go through our data deletion page. When you ask us to delete your information, we delete it from our systems and from the providers we use. We respond within 30 days.

9. Children

Our service is aimed at adults and at students whose parents or guardians contact us. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
