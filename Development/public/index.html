Bonjour, je suis Saloni

French changed my life — and yours could be next. I simply started learning French on weekends, just to use my free time better. What seemed like a small step turned into a life-changing decision.

French helped me land all three of my jobs, and in 2023 I achieved C1 in TEF Canada. Since then I've been helping school students and professionals unlock the same opportunities — whether it's exams like TEF or career and PR goals.

Learning a new language doesn't just add a skill — it opens doors you didn't even know existed.
